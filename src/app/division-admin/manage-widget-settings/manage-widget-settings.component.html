info Weighted random selection

Up to {{ activePartnersMaxDisplay }} partners are displayed on the home widget at a time. Each refresh picks partners at random without repeating within the same view. Partners with higher total engagement for the selected school year are more likely to appear.

Weighted chance per draw P(partner) = Epartner ÷ ΣEremaining

E is total engagement amount. The shuffle button and automatic rotation both use this weighted random selection.
